Privacy Policy

Goat Farm Manager (com.goatfarm.app.goat_farm_manage)
Effective date: October 26, 2026
Summary: Goat Farm Manager is an offline-first app. Your farm data is stored on your own device. We do not run servers that collect your data, and we do not sell your personal information.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how the Goat Farm Manager mobile application (“the App”, “we”, “us”) handles information. By installing and using the App, you agree to this policy.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect, transmit, or store your farm records on any server we control. We do not require an account, email address, or registration to use the App.

3. Data Stored on Your Device

The App stores the following information locally on your device, in a local database, so you can manage your farm:

  • Farm profile details you enter (farm name, address, phone number, currency and unit preferences).
  • Goat herd records (ear tags, names, breeds, weights, pedigree, breeding and kidding events).
  • Milk production, feed, health, vaccination, FAMACHA and drug withdrawal records.
  • Financial records, sales invoices, buyer information you type in, and receipts.
  • Your optional 4-digit security PIN and the 3-month edit and delete audit log.

This data stays on your device unless you choose to export or share it (see Section 5). We cannot access it, and we cannot recover it if you lose your device without a backup.

4. Permissions

  • Storage / files: used to save automatic daily backups (GoatFarmManager_Backups), CSV exports, and PDF reports, and to let you choose a backup file to restore.
  • Internet / network: not needed for core features. It is used only for optional features such as Google Play purchases, advertising (if enabled), or when you choose to export to a cloud drive.

5. Data You Choose to Share or Export

You control any export. When you generate a PDF invoice or report, export CSV or JSON files, print to a Wi-Fi printer, share via WhatsApp or email, or save a backup to Google Drive, that data is handled by the app or service you select and is subject to that service’s own privacy policy. Backup files are not sent to us.

6. Third-Party Services

The App may use the following third-party services, which may collect information under their own policies:

  • Google Play Billing: processes in-app purchases and subscriptions (monthly, yearly, lifetime). We do not receive or store your payment card details. See Google’s Privacy Policy.
  • Google Drive (optional): only when you manually export a backup to your own account.
  • Advertising (if shown in the free version): ad providers such as Google AdMob may collect device identifiers, such as the advertising ID, and usage data to serve ads. You can reset or opt out of personalized ads in your Android device settings. Purchasing a premium option removes ads.

7. Security

Your data is stored locally on your device. The optional 4-digit PIN restricts deletions, edits to financial records, goat departures, restores, and audit log access. We recommend keeping your device locked, enabling backups, and keeping your PIN private. No method of electronic storage is completely secure.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Your data remains on your device until you delete it. You can remove records inside the App, or delete all data by uninstalling the App and deleting the GoatFarmManager_Backups folder and any exported files you created. Because we hold no copy of your data, we cannot delete it on your behalf.

9. Children’s Privacy

The App is intended for farmers and agricultural users and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Your Rights

Because your data is stored only on your device, you already have full access and control over it. If you have questions about your privacy rights under laws such as GDPR or CCPA, contact us at the address below.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Continued use of the App after changes means you accept the updated policy.
